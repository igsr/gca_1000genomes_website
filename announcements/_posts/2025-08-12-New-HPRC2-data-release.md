---
title: "HPRC2 data release"
---

Sequence data from the Human Pangenome Reference Consortium ([HPRC](https://humanpangenome.org/hprc-data-release-2/)) is now indexed in IGSR. HPRC Release 2 includes sequencing data and high-quality phased genomes from over 200 individuals

More information and links to data files are available on the [HPRC2 data collection page](https://www.internationalgenome.org/data-portal/data-collection/hprc2)
